## Definitions going forward

- **Published episode:** present in RSS with a reachable positive-length `audio/mpeg` enclosure.
- **On-time episode:** published within two hours of the first scheduled attempt.
- **Material correction:** a post-publication change to a factual claim, attribution, number, or described capability.
- **Activated CastForge show:** a non-owner repository that publishes three consecutive valid episodes with CastForge.

GitHub stars measure awareness. Activated external shows are the adoption outcome.
